Voice test

Listen to OpenAI’s French voices on the forms that are easiest to get wrong, and keep the ones that sound like a real French speaker. This page is separate from your verb app and never touches your progress.

Your key

The key stays in this browser and goes only to OpenAI. It is not saved in this file or sent anywhere else. Delete this page from your repo when you have finished testing.

Voice instructions and speed

Changing these makes new clips, which costs a little more. Clips you have already heard are reused until you change them.

The forms

These are chosen because voices often stumble on them: liaisons (ils ont against ils sont), silent endings, and short forms with little context.

Voices

Play runs the chosen form in that voice. All 12 runs every form in that voice, so you can judge it in one go. Mark each voice Keep or Drop as you go.
